# Three Lead-Generation Systems

## 1. Funnel Gap Monitoring

A stateful audit system that starts from a company website, discovers popups,
forms, lead magnets and embedded booking flows, performs an approved
transaction, then monitors delivery and follow-up for ten days.

Its goal is to produce evidence-backed outreach opportunities based on real
technical or marketing gaps. Companies without a verified gap never enter an
outreach sequence.

## 2. Technology Stack Sourcing

A reusable skill with two modes:

- Discover companies using specified technologies.
- Enrich known companies with their technology stacks.

It is sourcing and enrichment rather than a live signal. Its output supports
integration, automation and AI-brain offers and can feed later contact-finding
and outreach pipelines.

## 3. Pipeline Engine Hiring Intent

A LinkedIn Jobs collector that finds companies actively hiring SDR, BDR and
related pipeline roles. It reads each job, captures the company website and
LinkedIn page, extracts compensation when available, and deduplicates by
LinkedIn job ID.

On demand it behaves like a sourcing skill. When scheduled continuously it
becomes a live hiring-intent signal and can feed an autonomous outreach system.

## System boundary

The signal and skill components detect or source opportunities. Contact
finding, campaign enrollment and cold-email sending remain separate downstream
systems.
