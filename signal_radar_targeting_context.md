# Signal Radar Targeting Context

This is the fixed targeting context for the first signal tests.

## 1. What are we selling?

AIessentials sells two main services:

### AI Operating System

A custom AI and automation system built around the client's existing tools and workflows. It removes repetitive work, connects business data, creates useful daily reports, and helps the company operate without adding unnecessary staff.

### 24/7 Pipeline Engine

A managed or build-and-handoff outbound system for B2B companies. It includes targeting, lead lists, cold email infrastructure, follow-ups, CRM integration, reporting, and booking.

The first outreach offer can be a free 30-minute AI Revenue Leak Report. It shows the company's top three time or revenue leaks and gives them a 90-day action plan.

## 2. Who normally buys it?

Primary buyers:

- Founder
- Co-founder
- CEO
- Agency owner
- Managing director
- Head of Operations
- Operations lead

Founders and owners are the best first targets because they feel both the operations and revenue problems directly.

## 3. What company type and size should we target?

Target company types:

- B2B agencies
- B2B service companies
- Lead generation agencies
- Marketing agencies
- Sales and outbound agencies
- Consulting companies
- Other operations-heavy small businesses with repeatable work

Target size:

- Main range: 5 to 50 employees
- Best starting range: 5 to 20 employees
- Small companies are the priority

Good companies have repeatable sales, reporting, admin, delivery, or operations work that could be automated. They should also have a clear service or offer they already sell.

Avoid companies with no stable workflow, no clear offer, or no ability to handle more customers.

## 4. Which countries should we target?

- United States
- Canada

## 5. Who is the main competitor?

- Ben.AI

We can also watch complaints or switching signals related to other AI automation agencies, outbound agencies, lead generation services, and sales automation providers.

## 6. What problems do we solve?

Main problems:

- Too much repetitive manual work
- Founders spending their time on admin and coordination
- Data being copied between tools by hand
- Slow follow-up with leads
- Inconsistent pipeline and unpredictable meetings
- Too much dependence on referrals
- Weak or missing CRM processes
- Disconnected tools and complicated workflows
- Manual reporting and daily status checking
- Hiring people to solve work that automation could handle
- Confusion about how to use AI inside the business
- Automation providers building a system and then disappearing

The desired result is a simpler business with less manual work, better visibility, faster follow-up, and a more predictable pipeline.

## Initial Test Rule

Each signal test will use only one lead or one company at a time.

All tests will be read-only. We will not send messages, request connections, or change anything on LinkedIn.
